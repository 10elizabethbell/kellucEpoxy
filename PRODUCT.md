# Kelluc Epoxy — product

- **Platform:** web
- **Stack:** static single-file HTML/CSS/JS (`index.html`), images in `img/`, hosted on GitHub Pages
- **Live:** https://10elizabethbell.github.io/kellucEpoxy/

## Users
- Homeowners in Windsor-Essex County (Ontario) and surrounding areas with a garage, basement, porch, steps or front entrance in bare or tired concrete. They mostly arrive from Facebook (group posts, the Kelluc Epoxy page) on a phone, inside Facebook's in-app browser.
- The owner of Kelluc Epoxy (name unknown), who receives the site for free.

## Purpose
Turn a Facebook-only epoxy business into one that has a real home on the web: show the work, say where they work, and get the visitor into a free-quote conversation. Today every quote happens in Facebook Messenger, so that is the contact channel.

## Positioning
Local epoxy and concrete floor coatings, flake finishes and custom colours, with free, no-obligation quotes. Their own lines: "Transform your concrete into something you'll love." (Facebook page description), "Durable • Beautiful • Built to last" (logo), "Transforming concrete, one floor at a time!" (post sign-off).

## Capabilities and constraints
- Contact is Facebook Messenger only: no phone or email is published anywhere (Muse, 2026-10-20). The quote form composes a message and hands off to Messenger (`m.me/100072376201501`), with a copy-message fallback.
- No phone number, so no `tel:`/`sms:` links; the header's phone slot is empty until the owner supplies one.
- One real work photo is available (front entrance steps, flake finish). The rest of their work is on the Facebook page.
- No reviews, prices, hours, owner name or years in business. None are shown.

## Brand commitments
- Logo: red triquetra over a black, silver-ringed badge with "KELLUC EPOXY" and "Durable • Beautiful • Built to last".
- Red is the one accent (fill). The page ground is never black: graphite navy and deep navy stand in for the logo's black.
- Canadian spelling (colours).
- Voice: "we" (the posts say "we're still working", "we're booking"); upbeat but not shouty.

## Evidence on hand
- Facebook page "Kelluc Epoxy": 582 followers (og description, 2026-10-20).
- Group post 2026-10-20, "Essex County Contractor Referrals": garage floor and front entrance, flake finish. Only the front-entrance after photo was retrievable.
- Seasonal: "We're booking indoor projects throughout the fall and winter."

## Principles
- Phone first; the first phone screen says what, where, and how to get a quote.
- Only real facts. Inferences are marked in `HANDOFF.md`.
- One contact channel done well beats several half-wired ones.
